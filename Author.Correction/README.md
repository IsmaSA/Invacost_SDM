# Corrigendum – Soto et al. 2025

This document provides a summary of the main corrections made to the paper by Soto et al.

### Main Changes

We identified two errors in the data files used in the original paper, for which I sincerely apologise.  

### Corrections

1. **Human population predictor (United Kingdom)**
   - A typo was found in three interpolated entries, affecting two species (*Tuta absoluta* and *Pontederia crassipes*).

2. **Interpolation of economic costs within native ranges**
   - Costs were incorrectly interpolated for species native to some areas of the same country (e.g., *Pacifastacus leniusculus* in the United States).

### Updated Results (see correction for full details)

- The **global cost estimate** decreased from **USD 2,215 billion** to **USD 787.31 billion** for the 162-species subset.  
- This still represents a **519% increase** over reported costs in InvaCost.  
- **Damage costs** account for **94% of the total**, consistent with the original findings.    

### Table 1. Corrected global estimates (USD billion; % increase relative to InvaCost)

| Category   | Corrected Cost | % Increase vs InvaCost |
|------------|----------------|-------------------------|
| Global     | 787.31         | +519%                  |
| Damage     | 740.91         | +529%                  |
| Management | 43.38          | +378%                  |

**These corrections do not alter the main conclusions of the paper**
